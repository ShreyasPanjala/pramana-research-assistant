An embedding in AI is a list of numbers—called a vector—that represents the meaning of data like text, images, or audio. It acts like coordinates on a map of meaning, placing similar concepts close together and different concepts far apart.How Embeddings WorkTranslation to numbers: Computers only understand math, so an AI model converts raw words or pixels into a long sequence of numbers.Capturing context: Words with similar meanings (like "dog" and "puppy") get numbers that point to nearby spots in a mathematical space. Words that mean different things (like "dog" and "banana") point far apart.Multidimensional mapping: These lists can have hundreds of dimensions, where each number measures a hidden trait like age, gender, or tone
